Keep your collection log synced so your drops count

  1. Before the event starts: you should
    • check you're listed in the Runecraft TempleOSRS group, and ask a mod to add you if not, since the board only reads that group;
    • install the TempleOSRS RuneLite plugin and turn on Automatically sync Collection Log and Auto-Update in its settings;
    • turn on the in-game Collection log new addition chat message (search "collection" in the game settings), so new items reach TempleOSRS without opening the log;
    • open your collection log and click through every page once, so TempleOSRS records everything you already have.
  2. During the event: if you get a bingo drop you already had in your log, you should open that page of your collection log again, since repeat drops only reach TempleOSRS once you do.
  3. For every bingo drop: you should screenshot it with the Clan Events plugin on and submit it to the mods, since screenshots are the primary verification for tiles.

Rules and Info

The board updates itself from TempleOSRS, so it can only see what your collection log tells it.

How to winEach tile is 1 point, and each completed line is 3 more. A line is any row, column or diagonal of 5 tiles. The team with the most points when time runs out wins. On a tie, the team that reached that score first wins.
Before the startBe in the Runecraft TempleOSRS group (only a leader can add you, so ask a mod). Install the TempleOSRS RuneLite plugin and, in its settings, turn on Automatically sync Collection Log (Collection Log section) and Auto-Update (Ranks section). In the game settings, turn on the Collection log new addition chat message. Then open your collection log and click through every page once.
First-time dropsIf the item is new to your collection log, it reaches the board on its own, as long as Automatically sync Collection Log and the new addition chat message are on.
Repeat dropsIf you already had the item, the board only sees your count go up. Open that item's collection log page after the drop so Temple picks up the new count. If you're unsure, open it anyway.
Drop timesNew items keep the time you got them. Repeat drops get the time your log last synced, so open the page soon after the drop. Drops from before the end that sync late still count for 3 hours after it.
KC and XPProgress charts and the Runecraft tile use your TempleOSRS stats, which Auto-Update refreshes when you log out. On long grinds, log out now and then.
ScreenshotsScreenshot every bingo drop with the Clan Events plugin on and submit it to the mods. Screenshots are the primary verification; the board tracks progress live.
Not in the group, no pointsThe board only reads the Runecraft TempleOSRS group. If you're not in it, or your log isn't synced, nothing you get will count. Check your status in the Teams list.
