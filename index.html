Rules and Info

The board updates itself from TempleOSRS, so it can only see what your collection log tells it.

How to winThe first team to complete a full line wins. A line is any row, column or diagonal of 5 tiles.
Before the startInstall the TempleOSRS RuneLite plugin. In its settings, turn on Automatically sync Collection Log (Collection Log section) and Auto-Update (Ranks section). Then open your collection log and click through every page, so Temple records what you already have.
First-time dropsIf the item is new to your collection log, it reaches the board on its own, as long as Automatically sync Collection Log is on.
Repeat dropsIf you already had the item, the board only sees your count go up. Open that item's collection log page after the drop so Temple picks up the new count. If you're unsure, open it anyway.
Drop timesEach tile shows when the board first saw the drop, not the exact minute it happened. If two teams finish a line close together, organisers decide the winner by hand.
KC and XPProgress charts and the Runecraft tile use your TempleOSRS stats, which Auto-Update refreshes when you log out. On long grinds, log out now and then.
Screenshots, just in caseKeep the clan event plugin on and screenshot every bingo drop. If the board misses one, organisers can check it.
No Temple profile, no pointsIf Temple can't find your RSN, nothing you get will count. Check the Teams list.
